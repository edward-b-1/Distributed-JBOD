Scrub

Every node reads every record and block on its own devices against their checksums, then the coordinator checks that record copies agree and every listed device has its shard.

Drain

One pass over every version on a draining device, moving each shard to another device. Mark the device draining on the Overview tab first. Rerun after fixing whatever was skipped, then remove the device.

Erasure coding scheme

New writes use the new scheme; existing objects keep theirs and stay readable. djbod cluster reencode rewrites them. Refused if fewer devices are active than k+m.
